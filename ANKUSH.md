# Ankush

This file defines how Ankush himself should be represented when he appears in a thumbnail.

## Core Principle

Ankush is a subject within the composition, not a mandatory thumbnail formula.

His treatment should change according to the concept.

He may appear:

- naturally photographed
- cinematically photographed
- conceptually integrated
- graphically manipulated
- partially obscured
- blurred or out of focus
- in other treatments when the concept requires it

Do not force a recurring facial expression, pose, crop, or treatment.

## Identity

When Ankush's face is visible, preserve his recognizable identity.

Do not unnecessarily alter:

- facial structure
- defining features
- skin characteristics
- apparent age

Stylization is acceptable when conceptually justified, but identity should remain recognizable.

## AI-Generated Scenes

When Ankush needs to exist inside an AI-generated or constructed environment, build the environment and composition first.

Do not repeatedly regenerate Ankush's face throughout the creative iteration process if doing so causes his identity to degrade.

Instead:

1. Establish the scene.
2. Establish composition, pose, perspective, scale, and lighting.
3. Iterate on the environment and overall thumbnail.
4. Introduce a real camera photograph of Ankush as the identity reference at the final replacement stage.
5. Integrate the resulting subject into the scene.

The final result should look as though Ankush belongs naturally within the photograph.

His lighting, perspective, scale, colour, focus, grain, and other photographic characteristics should agree with the destination scene.

Avoid both extremes:

- pasted-on photograph
- obviously AI-generated version of Ankush

## Real Photography

When using an actual photograph of Ankush, preserve the useful characteristics of the original image unless the concept requires modification.

Do not automatically apply cinematic grading, blur, sharpening, or other treatments.

Treat the photograph as source material that must fit the composition.

## Final Rule

Ankush's presence should serve the thumbnail's idea.

Do not include his face merely because having a face is considered a YouTube thumbnail convention.
